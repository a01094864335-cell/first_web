# Full-audit coordinator outfit sample

Reviewed 21 actual composite contact sheets generated by `tools/render_full_combo_audit.py`: six deliberately dense cross-slot outfits × seven source motions × four directions × four frames = **672 composed poses**. Each sheet was opened and visually inspected. Selected representative images and exact selections are in `previews/full-audit/`; the generator reproduces all 21 sheets into a supplied output directory.

The six outfits combine old/new hair, four hat styles, all three eye shapes, lens colours, both added skin tones, long/short sleeves, skirts/trousers/shorts, all three shoes, bags/headphones and all four hand props. This is an additional sample, not all equipable permutations.

No new confirmed blocking anchor, clipping or layer-order defect was found in this sample. Camera/book disappearance during drinking and held-object rear occlusion are intentional. Dense bag + hand-prop combinations cover substantial portions of the front torso; large rear hair naturally hides some arm gestures. These are visibility/art-direction tradeoffs, not proof that every combination will be equally readable. Exact fingertip grasp and dark-eye contrast remain polish limitations already documented.

This review used rendered frames, not actual browser Canvas playback or in-game interaction. It does not replace the per-item exhaustive source-frame pass documented separately.
